Why We Exist

AI agents report on their own work. "Tests pass." "Fixed." "Deployed."
Sometimes the report and the record disagree.

Our approach: telemetry that records what an agent actually did, and evals that check what it says against that record. The check is a gate the agent can't reach, so a claim that doesn't match reality gets caught instead of believed.

Not because we don't trust agents.
Because trust requires evidence.

Our Approach

Where our work is headed. Two pieces that only work together: a record of what happened, and a check that holds the agent's claims to it.

Telemetry

A record of what the agent did: what it said, what it ran, what came back. Written somewhere the agent can't edit, so it can be checked later.

This is the direction of our work, not a product you can sign up for.

Evals with gates

Checks that compare an agent's claim with the record. "All tests pass" only counts if the run log says so. The gate sits outside the agent's reach, and its pass bar is set before the results come in.

Our research already works this way: each Verifiability Seam experiment commits its kill threshold before any data exists. See Vivarium Lab below.
